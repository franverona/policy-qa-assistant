# Water Backup and Sump Discharge Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Policies this endorsement attaches to

This endorsement can be attached to HomeShield (HM-HO-2024), CondoGuard (HM-CO-2024) and Landlord Dwelling (HM-DP-2024) policies. It is not available for HomeShield 2022 edition policies.

## Endorsement Terms

### 2 Coverage

We cover direct physical loss caused by water or waterborne material that backs up through sewers or drains, or that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump or related equipment, even if the overflow results from mechanical breakdown of the sump pump.

### 3 Limit of liability

The most we pay for all loss in one occurrence is the limit shown on the declarations page for this endorsement: $5,000, $10,000 or $25,000. This limit is part of, not in addition to, the Coverage A and Coverage C limits.

### 4 Deductible

Each loss under this endorsement is subject to a deductible of $1,000. This deductible applies instead of the all-peril deductible of the policy.

### 5 Not covered

This endorsement does not cover:

- Loss caused by flood or surface water, even if it enters through a drain or sump.
- Loss caused by your failure to keep the sump pump in working order or to fix a known blockage in your sewer line.
- The cost to repair or replace the sump pump, the sewer line or the drain itself.

### 6 Waiting period

Coverage under this endorsement begins 10 days after it is added to the policy, unless it is added at the policy's first issue or at renewal.
