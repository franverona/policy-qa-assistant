# Billing, Payments and Grace Periods

## Payment plans

Customers can pay in full, in two instalments, quarterly or monthly. Monthly and quarterly plans carry an instalment fee of $3 per instalment. Customers who pay in full or set up automatic payments from a bank account get a 3% discount on the premium. Card autopay does not qualify for the discount.

## Payment methods

We accept bank transfer (ACH), debit and credit cards, and cheques by post. We do not accept cash at any office. Card payments over $5,000 must be split or paid by bank transfer.

## Due dates and grace period

Each instalment is due on the date shown on the bill. If a payment is missed, the customer has a 10-day grace period from the due date, during which coverage continues. A notice of cancellation for non-payment is sent on day 1 of the grace period and takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on day 11 if the payment is still outstanding.

## Returned payments

A returned or declined payment carries a $25 fee. After two returned payments in 12 months, the customer must pay by debit card or bank transfer only, and autopay is switched off.

## Reinstatement after cancellation

If a policy was cancelled for non-payment, it can be reinstated without a gap in coverage if the full overdue amount is paid within 15 days of the cancellation date and the customer signs a no-loss statement confirming there were no losses since cancellation. After 15 days, the customer must apply for a new policy.

## Changing the billing date

Customers may move their due date once per policy term, by up to 15 days. Tier 1 agents can make this change.
