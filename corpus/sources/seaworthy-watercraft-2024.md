# Seaworthy Watercraft Policy

## Section 1 — Definitions

### 1.1 "Covered boat"

"Covered boat" means the boat shown on the declarations page, its permanently attached equipment, its motor and its trailer. We insure boats up to 32 feet in length. Personal watercraft such as jet skis are covered only if listed by name on the declarations page.

### 1.2 "Navigation limits"

"Navigation limits" means inland lakes and rivers of the United States and its coastal waters no more than 25 nautical miles from shore, unless the declarations page shows wider limits.

### 1.3 "Lay-up period"

"Lay-up period" means the period shown on the declarations page when the covered boat is stored out of the water and not in use. Unless a different period is shown, the lay-up period is December 1 to March 31.

## Section 2 — Physical Damage Coverage

### 2.1 Insuring agreement

We cover direct physical loss to a covered boat while it is afloat, on land or being transported, within the navigation limits, unless the loss is excluded in Section 4.

### 2.2 Valuation

If the declarations page shows "Agreed Value", we pay the agreed value for a total loss, less the deductible. If it shows "Actual Cash Value", we pay the actual cash value at the time of loss. Partial losses are paid at the cost to repair, with depreciation deducted for sails, canvas, batteries and outboard motors older than 5 years.

### 2.3 Personal effects

We cover fishing equipment, water-ski gear and personal effects on board up to $1,500 per occurrence. Money, jewelry and electronic devices that are not permanently installed are not covered.

### 2.4 Hurricane haul-out

If a named storm watch or warning is issued for the area where the covered boat is kept, we pay 50% of the cost to haul it out of the water, move it to a safer location or hire a licensed captain to move it, up to $1,000 per storm. No deductible applies.

## Section 3 — Liability and Other Coverages

### 3.1 Watercraft liability

We pay damages an insured is legally liable for because of bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership or use of a covered boat, up to the limit shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the limit is $300,000 per occurrence.

### 3.2 Water skier liability

Liability coverage applies to people being towed by a covered boat, such as water skiers and tubers, provided there is an observer on board in addition to the operator.

### 3.3 Towing and assistance

We pay up to $500 per occurrence for towing a disabled covered boat to the nearest place where repairs can be made, and for delivery of fuel, oil or parts. The cost of the fuel and parts is not covered.

### 3.4 Wreck removal

If a covered boat sinks or is wrecked and removal is required by law, we pay the cost of removal up to 10% of the physical damage limit, in addition to that limit.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Operation outside navigation limits

We do not cover loss or liability while the covered boat is outside the navigation limits.

### 4.2 Lay-up period

We do not cover loss while the covered boat is in the water or being used during the lay-up period, other than while being moved to or from storage.

### 4.3 Wear, tear and marine life

We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, osmosis or blistering, corrosion, electrolysis, or damage by marine life, insects or vermin.

### 4.4 Racing and charter

We do not cover a covered boat while used in a race or speed contest, or while rented or chartered to others for a fee.

### 4.5 Unqualified operator

We do not cover loss while the covered boat is operated by a person who does not hold any boating safety certificate required by the state where it is operated.

## Section 5 — Deductibles and Conditions

### 5.1 Deductible

Each physical damage loss is subject to the deductible shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the deductible is 1% of the agreed value or actual cash value, with a minimum of $500. For loss caused by a named storm, the deductible is 5% with a minimum of $1,000.

### 5.2 Your duties after loss

You must give us notice no later than 30 days after a loss, take reasonable steps to protect the covered boat from further damage and keep damaged parts for our inspection.

### 5.3 Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel for non-payment of premium with at least 10 days' notice. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
