# Cancellations and Refunds

## Customer-requested cancellation

Any customer may cancel at any time. Cancellation of property, auto, motorcycle, watercraft and umbrella policies can be requested by phone, but must be confirmed in writing (email or signed form) by the named insured before it is processed. RentSafe renters policies are the exception: a phone request from the verified named insured is enough. The cancellation date cannot be earlier than the date we receive the request, unless the customer shows proof that replacement insurance started earlier.

## Refund calculation

Refunds of unearned premium are calculated pro rata for all products. Harborline Mutual does not charge a cancellation fee or use short-rate tables. Instalment fees already paid are not refunded.

## Free look periods

- PawGuard pet: 30 days from the start date, full refund if no claim has been paid.
- Voyager travel: 14 days from purchase, full refund if the customer has not departed and no claim has been made. After that, travel premium is not refundable.
- Other products have no free look period; refunds are pro rata from day one.

## Refund timing

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days of the cancellation being processed. If the original card has expired, we send a cheque to the mailing address on file.

## Lender and mortgagee notices

If a mortgagee or lienholder is listed on the policy, we must send them notice of cancellation at least 10 days before the cancellation date. Tell the customer that their lender will be informed.

## Retention

Before processing a cancellation, Tier 1 agents should ask the reason and, if it is price, offer a review of deductibles and discounts. Do not delay a cancellation if the customer has already decided.
