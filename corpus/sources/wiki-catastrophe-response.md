# Catastrophe Event Procedures

## What is a catastrophe event

The Claims Operations team declares a catastrophe (CAT) event when a single storm, wildfire or other event is expected to cause more than 500 claims. The event gets a code such as CAT-2024-03, shown on the agent dashboard banner. Agents must add the CAT code to every FNOL related to the event.

## Binding moratorium

While a CAT event is active, no new property policies, coverage increases or new endorsements can be bound for addresses in the affected postcodes. This also applies to water backup endorsement HM-END-04. Quotes can still be given, but must say they cannot be bound until the moratorium is lifted. Renewals of existing policies are not affected.

## Emergency advances during a CAT event

During a declared CAT event, Tier 2 agents may approve emergency living expense advances up to $2,500 per household, instead of the usual $1,500. The advance is deducted from the final Coverage D payment. Tier 1 agents still have no payment authority.

## Deadline extensions

For losses in a declared CAT event, the deadline for reporting a loss is extended by 30 days beyond the deadline in the policy. Proof of loss deadlines are extended by the same amount. Tell customers about the extension if they are worried about reporting late.

## Prioritising calls

During a CAT event, take FNOL first and leave general questions for later. A home that is uninhabitable is P1, as on the Claims Escalation page. Ask whether the customer has somewhere safe to stay tonight on every CAT call.

## Fraud awareness

Contractors who travel to disaster areas sometimes ask customers to sign over their claim. Remind customers not to sign an assignment of benefits without reading it, and that they can always speak to their adjuster first.
