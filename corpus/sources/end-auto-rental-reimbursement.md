# Auto Rental Reimbursement Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Policies this endorsement attaches to

This endorsement can be attached to DriveWell personal auto policies (HM-PA-2023 and HM-PA-2024) for any covered auto that has collision or comprehensive coverage.

## Endorsement Terms

### 2 Coverage

If a covered auto cannot be driven, or is in the repair shop, because of a loss covered under collision or comprehensive, we reimburse the cost of renting a substitute vehicle, or of taxis, rideshares and public transport you use instead.

### 3 Limits

The declarations page shows one of the following options:

- Option 1: $40 per day, up to $1,200 per loss.
- Option 2: $60 per day, up to $1,800 per loss.

### 4 Period of coverage

Coverage begins 24 hours after the covered auto becomes undrivable or is left at the repair shop, whichever is earlier. It ends when the covered auto is repaired, or, if we declare a total loss, 5 days after we make an offer to settle the loss.

### 5 Not covered

We do not pay for fuel, mileage charges, rental company damage waivers, or delays caused by you, such as not authorising repairs. Mechanical breakdown is not a covered loss under this endorsement.

### 6 No deductible

No deductible applies to this endorsement.
