# Harbor Personal Umbrella Policy

## Section 1 — Agreement and Definitions

### 1.1 Agreement

In return for the premium you pay, Harborline Mutual Insurance Company ("we", "us", "our") agrees to provide personal liability insurance in excess of the underlying insurance described in this policy.

### 1.2 "Underlying insurance"

"Underlying insurance" means the auto, homeowners, renters, condominium, watercraft and landlord liability policies listed on the declarations page, whether issued by us or by another insurer.

### 1.3 "Retained limit"

"Retained limit" means the amount you pay for a loss that is covered by this policy but not by any underlying insurance. The retained limit is $1,000 per occurrence.

## Section 2 — Coverage

### 2.1 Insuring agreement

We pay damages in excess of the underlying insurance, or in excess of the retained limit where no underlying insurance applies, for bodily injury, property damage or personal injury for which an insured becomes legally liable.

### 2.2 Limit of liability

The limit of liability is shown on the declarations page and is available in increments of $1,000,000, up to $5,000,000. The limit is the most we pay for any one occurrence, regardless of the number of insureds or claims.

### 2.3 Personal injury

"Personal injury" means injury arising out of false arrest, malicious prosecution, wrongful eviction, libel, slander or invasion of privacy. Posts on social media are included.

### 2.4 Defense

We defend any suit for damages covered by this policy that is not defended by underlying insurance. Defense costs are paid in addition to the limit of liability.

## Section 3 — Required Underlying Insurance

### 3.1 Minimum underlying limits

You must keep the following underlying insurance in force for the whole policy period:

- Auto liability: $250,000 per person and $500,000 per accident for bodily injury, and $100,000 property damage, or a $500,000 combined single limit.
- Homeowners, renters or condominium personal liability: $300,000 per occurrence.
- Watercraft liability, if you own a boat over 26 feet or with more than 50 horsepower: $300,000 per occurrence.
- Landlord liability, for each rental dwelling: $300,000 per occurrence.

### 3.2 Failure to maintain

If the underlying insurance is not maintained at the required limits, we pay only the amount we would have paid if it had been maintained. You are responsible for the gap.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Business and professional activities

We do not cover liability arising out of business pursuits or professional services, except for volunteer work for a non-profit organisation.

### 4.2 Intentional acts

We do not cover injury or damage expected or intended by an insured.

### 4.3 Aircraft and large watercraft

We do not cover liability arising out of the ownership or use of aircraft, or of watercraft longer than 40 feet.

### 4.4 Communicable disease

We do not cover liability arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured.

## Section 5 — Conditions

### 5.1 Notice

You must give us written notice of any occurrence that may result in a claim under this policy as soon as practical, and no later than 30 days after you learn of it.

### 5.2 Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel with at least 30 days' notice if any required underlying insurance is cancelled or not renewed. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
