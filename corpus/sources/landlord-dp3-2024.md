# Landlord Dwelling Policy

## Section 1 — Agreement and Definitions

### 1.1 Agreement

In return for the premium you pay, Harborline Mutual Insurance Company ("we", "us", "our") agrees to provide the insurance described in this policy for a one- to four-family dwelling that you own and rent to others.

### 1.2 "Described location"

"Described location" means the dwelling, other structures and grounds at the address shown on the declarations page.

### 1.3 "Tenant"

"Tenant" means a person who occupies the dwelling under a written lease of 30 days or longer.

## Section 2 — Property Coverages

### 2.1 Coverage A — Dwelling

We cover the dwelling at the described location, including attached structures and building equipment, fixtures and appliances you own that are used to service the dwelling.

### 2.2 Coverage B — Other Structures

We cover other structures at the described location up to 10% of the Coverage A limit.

### 2.3 Coverage C — Landlord's Personal Property

We cover personal property you own that is used to maintain or service the described location, such as lawn equipment and appliances you supply to tenants, up to $5,000. We do not cover property owned by tenants.

### 2.4 Coverage D — Fair Rental Value

If a covered loss makes the dwelling unfit to rent, we pay the fair rental value you lose, less any expenses that stop while it is unfit, for the shortest time required to repair it but no longer than 12 months. We do not pay for rent lost because a tenant stops paying or leaves for reasons unrelated to a covered loss.

## Section 3 — Perils Insured Against

### 3.1 Dwelling and other structures

We insure against direct physical loss to property described in Coverages A and B unless the loss is excluded in Section 4.

### 3.2 Vandalism by tenants

We cover vandalism and malicious mischief caused by a tenant up to $10,000 per occurrence. We do not cover theft by a tenant or wear and tear caused by a tenant.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Flood and earth movement

We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, earthquake, landslide or any other earth movement.

### 4.2 Vacancy

We do not cover loss caused by vandalism, glass breakage, water damage or freezing if the dwelling has been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days immediately before the loss. A dwelling between tenants that is being actively marketed for rent and inspected at least every 14 days is not considered vacant for the first 90 days.

### 4.3 Short-term rentals

We do not cover any loss that occurs while the dwelling is rented for fewer than 30 consecutive days, including rentals through home-sharing platforms.

### 4.4 Wear, tear and maintenance

We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, mold, pests, or continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of 14 days or more.

## Section 5 — Liability Coverage

### 5.1 Premises liability

We pay damages you are legally liable for because of bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership or maintenance of the described location, up to the limit shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the limit is $300,000 per occurrence.

### 5.2 Not covered

Liability coverage does not apply to claims made by tenants for return of security deposits, claims for discrimination or wrongful eviction, or injury caused by lead paint.

## Section 6 — Deductibles and Conditions

### 6.1 Deductible

Each loss under Section 2 is subject to the deductible shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the deductible is $1,500. For windstorm or hail, the deductible is 2% of the Coverage A limit.

### 6.2 Your duties after loss

You must give us notice no later than 60 days after the date of loss, provide a copy of the current lease, and give us access to the dwelling for inspection.

### 6.3 Loss settlement

Covered losses to the dwelling are settled at replacement cost if repairs are completed within 12 months. Otherwise we pay actual cash value.

### 6.4 Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel for non-payment of premium with at least 10 days' notice, or for any other permitted reason with at least 30 days' notice. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
