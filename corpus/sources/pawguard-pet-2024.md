# PawGuard Pet Health Policy

## Section 1 — Definitions

### 1.1 "Covered pet"

"Covered pet" means the dog or cat named on the declarations page. A pet must be at least 8 weeks old and younger than 14 years at enrollment. A covered pet stays covered at renewal regardless of age.

### 1.2 "Pre-existing condition"

"Pre-existing condition" means any illness or injury that first showed signs or symptoms before the coverage start date or during a waiting period, whether or not it was diagnosed by a veterinarian.

### 1.3 "Curable condition"

"Curable condition" means a pre-existing condition, such as an ear infection or vomiting, that has been cured and free of symptoms and treatment for 180 days. A curable condition is no longer treated as pre-existing after those 180 days. Cruciate ligament conditions are never treated as curable.

## Section 2 — Coverage

### 2.1 Accident and illness

We reimburse eligible veterinary expenses for the diagnosis and treatment of an accident or illness that first occurs after the waiting period, including exams, diagnostic tests, surgery, hospitalisation, prescription medication and emergency care.

### 2.2 Hereditary and chronic conditions

We cover hereditary and congenital conditions, such as hip dysplasia, and chronic conditions, such as diabetes, if no signs appeared before the coverage start date or during a waiting period.

### 2.3 Annual limit

The most we reimburse in a policy year is the annual limit shown on the declarations page: $5,000, $10,000 or unlimited.

### 2.4 Reimbursement and deductible

We reimburse the reimbursement percentage shown on the declarations page (70%, 80% or 90%) of eligible expenses after you have paid the annual deductible. The annual deductible is $250 unless the declarations page shows $100 or $500. It applies once per policy year, not per condition.

## Section 3 — Waiting Periods

### 3.1 Standard waiting periods

Coverage starts after the following waiting periods, counted from the policy start date:

- Accidents: 2 days.
- Illnesses: 14 days.
- Cruciate ligament conditions and hip dysplasia: 6 months.

### 3.2 Waiver for transferred coverage

We waive the waiting periods for illnesses and accidents if the covered pet had comparable coverage with another insurer that ended no more than 30 days before this policy started.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Not covered

We do not cover:

- Pre-existing conditions, except curable conditions after the 180-day period.
- Routine and preventive care, such as vaccinations, flea and tick prevention, dental cleaning, spaying and neutering.
- Breeding, pregnancy and whelping.
- Cosmetic procedures, such as tail docking, ear cropping and declawing.
- Food, including prescription diets.
- Grooming, boarding and training.

### 4.2 Wellness add-on

Routine and preventive care is covered only if the Wellness add-on is shown on the declarations page. It reimburses up to $450 per policy year for vaccinations, an annual exam, dental cleaning and flea, tick and heartworm prevention, with no deductible.

## Section 5 — Claims and Conditions

### 5.1 Submitting a claim

You must submit a claim with an itemised invoice from a licensed veterinarian within 180 days of the date of treatment. We may ask for the pet's medical records from the last 12 months before enrollment.

### 5.2 Claim payment

We process complete claims within 10 business days and pay you by direct deposit or cheque. We can pay the veterinarian directly if they agree and you ask before treatment.

### 5.3 Free look period

You may cancel within 30 days of the policy start date for a full refund of premium, provided no claim has been paid.

### 5.4 Cancellation

After the free look period you may cancel at any time and any unearned premium is refunded pro rata. We may cancel for non-payment of premium with at least 10 days' notice. We will not cancel or refuse to renew because your pet made claims.
