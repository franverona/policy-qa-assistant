# Scheduled Personal Property Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Policies this endorsement attaches to

This endorsement can be attached to HomeShield, RentSafe and CondoGuard policies. It insures valuable items that are individually listed, with an agreed amount, on the schedule attached to the declarations page.

## Endorsement Terms

### 2 Classes of property

Items can be scheduled in the following classes: jewelry and watches; furs; fine art; cameras; musical instruments; silverware; stamps and coins; golf equipment; and bicycles worth more than $1,500.

### 3 Coverage

We cover scheduled items against direct physical loss anywhere in the world, including mysterious disappearance and accidental loss, such as a ring lost down a drain. The special limits of liability in the policy do not apply to scheduled items.

### 4 Valuation

We pay the smallest of the amount shown on the schedule for the item, the cost to repair the item, or the cost to replace it with a substantially identical item. For fine art, we pay the amount shown on the schedule for a total loss.

### 5 Deductible

No deductible applies to loss of a scheduled item.

### 6 Newly acquired property

Newly acquired property of a class already scheduled is covered automatically for 30 days, up to 25% of the total scheduled amount for that class or $10,000, whichever is less. You must report the item to us within 30 days and pay any additional premium from the date you acquired it.

### 7 Appraisals

Any item scheduled for more than $5,000 needs a written appraisal or a sales receipt dated within the past 2 years. We may ask for a new appraisal every 5 years.

### 8 Not covered

We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, damage from repair or restoration work, breakage of fragile fine art unless caused by fire, theft or an accident to a vehicle carrying it, or items in the custody of a commercial shipper.
