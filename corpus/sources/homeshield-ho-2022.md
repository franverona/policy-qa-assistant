# HomeShield Homeowners Policy (2022 Edition)

## Section 1 — Agreement and Definitions

### 1.1 Agreement

In return for the premium you pay and your compliance with the conditions of this policy, Harborline Mutual Insurance Company ("we", "us", "our") agrees to provide the insurance described in this policy. This policy, the declarations page and any endorsements form the entire contract between you and us.

### 1.2 "You" and "your"

"You" and "your" mean the named insured shown on the declarations page and the spouse of the named insured if a resident of the same household.

### 1.3 "Residence premises"

"Residence premises" means the one-family dwelling, other structures and grounds at the address shown on the declarations page, where you reside.

### 1.4 "Sudden and accidental"

"Sudden and accidental" means an event that is unexpected and unintended from your standpoint. Damage that develops gradually is not sudden and accidental.

### 1.5 "Actual cash value"

"Actual cash value" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, less depreciation.

## Section 2 — Property Coverages

### 2.1 Coverage A — Dwelling

We cover the dwelling on the residence premises, including structures attached to the dwelling, and materials and supplies on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter or repair the dwelling.

### 2.2 Coverage B — Other Structures

We cover other structures on the residence premises that are separated from the dwelling by clear space, such as detached garages, sheds and fences. The limit for Coverage B is 10% of the Coverage A limit. We do not cover other structures used for business or rented to anyone.

### 2.3 Coverage C — Personal Property

We cover personal property owned or used by you while it is anywhere in the world. Personal property usually located at a residence other than the residence premises is limited to 10% of the Coverage C limit or $1,000, whichever is greater.

### 2.4 Special limits of liability

The following limits apply to each loss for property covered under Coverage C. These limits do not increase the Coverage C limit.

- $200 for money, bank notes, coins and stored value cards.
- $1,500 for securities, deeds, manuscripts, passports and tickets.
- $1,500 for theft of jewelry, watches, furs and precious stones.
- $2,500 for theft of firearms and related equipment.
- $2,500 for theft of silverware, goldware and pewterware.
- $2,500 for electronic devices and accessories used primarily for business.

### 2.5 Coverage D — Loss of Use

If a covered loss makes the residence premises unfit to live in, we cover additional living expenses: any necessary increase in living expenses you incur so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. Payment is for the shortest time required to repair or replace the damage, but no longer than 24 months from the date of loss.

## Section 3 — Perils Insured Against

### 3.1 Dwelling and other structures

We insure against direct physical loss to property described in Coverages A and B unless the loss is excluded in Section 4.

### 3.2 Personal property

We insure Coverage C property against direct physical loss caused by the following named perils: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot or civil commotion; aircraft; vehicles; smoke; vandalism; theft; falling objects; weight of ice, snow or sleet; and sudden and accidental discharge of water or steam from a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system.

### 3.3 Water damage from plumbing

We cover sudden and accidental discharge of water from within a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system or a household appliance. We also pay the reasonable cost to tear out and replace any part of the building necessary to repair the system or appliance, but not the cost to repair the system or appliance itself.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Flood

We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water or overflow of a body of water, whether or not driven by wind.

### 4.2 Earth movement

We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole or any other earth movement. We do cover ensuing fire or explosion caused by earth movement.

### 4.3 Wear, tear and maintenance

We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, rust, mold, wet or dry rot, or by your failure to maintain the property.

### 4.4 Continuous seepage or leakage

We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam over a period of 30 days or more from a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system or a household appliance.

### 4.5 Vacancy

We do not cover loss caused by vandalism, malicious mischief, glass breakage or freezing of plumbing if the dwelling has been vacant for more than 30 consecutive days immediately before the loss.

### 4.6 Business use

We do not cover property used in any business conducted on the residence premises, except as stated in clause 2.4.

## Section 5 — Deductibles

### 5.1 All-peril deductible

Each loss under Section 2 is subject to the all-peril deductible shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the deductible is $500. We pay only the part of the loss that exceeds the deductible.

### 5.2 Windstorm and hail deductible

For loss caused by windstorm or hail, the deductible is 1% of the Coverage A limit, with a minimum of $500. This deductible replaces the all-peril deductible for that loss.

## Section 6 — Conditions

### 6.1 Your duties after loss

After a loss to which this insurance may apply, you must:

- Give us notice as soon as practical.
- Notify the police in case of theft.
- Protect the property from further damage.
- Prepare an inventory of damaged personal property.
- Send us a signed proof of loss within 60 days of our request.

### 6.2 Loss settlement

Covered losses to the dwelling are settled at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation, up to the Coverage A limit, provided the repair or replacement is completed within 180 days of the date of loss. Personal property is settled at actual cash value unless endorsement HM-END-02 (Personal Property Replacement Cost) is attached.

### 6.3 Loss payment

We will pay you unless another payee is named in the policy. We will pay within 60 days after we receive your proof of loss and reach agreement with you.

### 6.4 Appraisal

If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may demand an appraisal in writing. Each party chooses an appraiser. The two appraisers choose an umpire. Agreement by any two of the three sets the amount of loss.

### 6.5 Suit against us

No action can be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with all policy provisions and the action is started within one year after the date of loss.

### 6.6 Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel for non-payment of premium by giving you at least 10 days' notice. When this policy has been in effect for 60 days or more, we may cancel for any other reason by giving you at least 30 days' notice. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
