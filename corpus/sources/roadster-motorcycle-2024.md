# Roadster Motorcycle Policy

## Part A — Definitions

### A.1 "Covered motorcycle"

"Covered motorcycle" means a motorcycle, scooter or moped shown on the declarations page with an engine displacement of 50cc or more, or an electric motorcycle with a top speed above 30 mph. Dirt bikes and vehicles not licensed for road use are not covered motorcycles.

### A.2 "Riding gear"

"Riding gear" means helmets, protective jackets, trousers, gloves and boots designed for motorcycle use.

## Part B — Liability Coverage

### B.1 Insuring agreement

We pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which an insured becomes legally responsible because of an accident arising out of the use of a covered motorcycle, up to the limits shown on the declarations page.

### B.2 Passenger liability

Liability coverage includes bodily injury to a passenger riding on a covered motorcycle, provided the motorcycle is designed to carry a passenger.

### B.3 Exclusions

We do not provide liability coverage while a covered motorcycle is used in a race, speed test or organised track event, or for delivery of goods for a fee.

## Part C — Physical Damage Coverage

### C.1 Collision and comprehensive

We pay for direct and accidental loss to a covered motorcycle, less the deductible shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the deductible is $500 for collision and $250 for comprehensive.

### C.2 Custom parts and equipment

We cover custom parts and equipment, such as chrome, saddlebags, custom paint and performance exhaust, up to $3,000 unless a higher limit is shown on the declarations page.

### C.3 Riding gear

We cover riding gear damaged in a covered collision up to $1,000 per occurrence, with no deductible. Theft of riding gear left on an unattended motorcycle is not covered.

### C.4 Total loss

If the cost to repair a covered motorcycle exceeds 70% of its actual cash value, we treat it as a total loss.

## Part D — Seasonal Lay-up

### D.1 Lay-up option

If the declarations page shows a lay-up period, liability and collision coverage are suspended during that period and only comprehensive coverage applies, for example for theft or fire while the motorcycle is stored. The premium reflects the reduced coverage.

### D.2 Riding during lay-up

If a covered motorcycle is ridden during the lay-up period, there is no liability or collision coverage for that use. You may end the lay-up early by calling us; coverage resumes from the time of the call.

## Part E — Duties and Conditions

### E.1 Duties after an accident or loss

You must give us notice no later than 30 days after an accident or loss and notify the police within 24 hours of a theft.

### E.2 Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by notifying us. We may cancel for non-payment of premium with at least 10 days' notice. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
