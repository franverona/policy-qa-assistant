# Personal Property Replacement Cost Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Policies this endorsement attaches to

This endorsement can be attached to HomeShield (HM-HO-2022 and HM-HO-2024) and CondoGuard (HM-CO-2024) policies. RentSafe renters policies already settle personal property at replacement cost and do not need it. When attached, this endorsement changes the Loss settlement condition of the policy for Coverage C.

## Endorsement Terms

### 2 Replacement cost settlement

Covered losses to personal property are settled at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation, up to the Coverage C limit. Replacement cost means the cost to replace the damaged item with a new item of comparable material, quality and function.

### 3 Property not eligible

Replacement cost settlement does not apply to the following, which remain settled at actual cash value:

- Antiques, fine art, paintings and similar items that cannot be replaced with new items.
- Memorabilia, souvenirs and collectors' items.
- Articles not maintained in good or workable condition.
- Articles that are outdated or obsolete and stored, not in use.

### 4 Payment before replacement

If the replacement cost of the entire loss is more than $500, we pay no more than actual cash value until the actual repair or replacement is completed. You must complete the replacement within 180 days of the date of loss to receive the difference between actual cash value and replacement cost.

### 5 Limit for a single item

The most we pay for any single item is the smallest of the replacement cost, the full cost of repair, or any special limit of liability that applies in the policy. This endorsement does not increase any special limit of liability.
