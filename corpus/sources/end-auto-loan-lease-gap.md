# Loan/Lease Gap Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Eligibility

This endorsement can be attached to DriveWell personal auto policy HM-PA-2024 for a covered auto that has both collision and comprehensive coverage, is financed or leased, and is no more than 3 model years old when the endorsement is added.

## Endorsement Terms

### 2 Coverage

If a covered auto is a total loss under collision or comprehensive, we pay the difference between the actual cash value we pay and the outstanding balance on your loan or lease at the date of loss.

### 3 Limit

The most we pay under this endorsement is 25% of the actual cash value of the covered auto.

### 4 Amounts not included

We do not pay:

- Overdue payments, late fees or penalties.
- Charges for excess mileage or wear on a leased vehicle.
- Extended warranties, credit insurance or service contracts financed with the loan.
- Any balance carried over from a previous loan or lease.
- Your physical damage deductible.

### 5 Ending coverage

This endorsement ends automatically when the loan or lease is paid off, or when the covered auto is replaced.
