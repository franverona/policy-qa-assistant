# Voyager Travel Protection Policy

## Section 1 — Definitions

### 1.1 "Trip"

"Trip" means the travel shown on your confirmation of coverage, with a departure and return date, lasting no more than 90 days.

### 1.2 "Trip cost"

"Trip cost" means the prepaid, non-refundable amounts for travel arrangements you insured, such as flights, accommodation and tours.

### 1.3 "Family member"

"Family member" means your spouse or domestic partner, children, parents, siblings, grandparents and grandchildren, including step and in-law relations.

## Section 2 — Trip Cancellation and Interruption

### 2.1 Trip cancellation

We reimburse up to 100% of the insured trip cost, to a maximum of $10,000 per person, if you cancel your trip before departure for a covered reason.

### 2.2 Trip interruption

We reimburse up to 150% of the insured trip cost if your trip is interrupted after departure for a covered reason. This includes the unused portion of the trip and additional transport to rejoin the trip or return home.

### 2.3 Covered reasons

Covered reasons are:

- Sickness, injury or death of you, a travelling companion or a family member.
- Your home being made uninhabitable by fire, flood or a natural disaster.
- A terrorist incident in a city on your itinerary within 30 days before departure.
- Your carrier ceasing all services for at least 24 hours because of severe weather.
- Being called to jury duty or subpoenaed.
- Involuntary termination of your employment after at least one year with the same employer.

### 2.4 Cancel for any reason

Cancellation for reasons not listed in clause 2.3, such as changing your mind, is not covered. This policy does not offer a cancel-for-any-reason option.

## Section 3 — Travel Delay and Baggage

### 3.1 Travel delay

If your trip is delayed by 6 hours or more for a covered reason, we reimburse reasonable meals, accommodation and local transport up to $150 per day, with a maximum of $750 per person.

### 3.2 Baggage loss

We reimburse permanent loss of or damage to your baggage during the trip up to $1,500 per person. The most we pay for any one item is $250, and the most we pay for all jewelry, cameras and electronics combined is $500.

### 3.3 Baggage delay

If your checked baggage is delayed by 12 hours or more, we reimburse essential items you buy up to $300 per person.

## Section 4 — Medical and Evacuation

### 4.1 Emergency medical

We reimburse emergency medical and dental treatment during the trip up to $50,000 per person. Dental treatment is limited to $750 and must be for the relief of sudden pain. This coverage is secondary to any other health insurance you have.

### 4.2 Emergency evacuation

We pay for emergency medical transport to the nearest adequate facility or home, when arranged by our assistance service, up to $250,000 per person.

### 4.3 Assistance service

Our 24-hour travel assistance line can arrange medical referrals, evacuation and replacement of lost travel documents. Evacuations not arranged by the assistance service are covered only up to what the assistance service would have paid.

## Section 5 — Exclusions

### 5.1 Pre-existing conditions

We do not cover loss caused by a pre-existing condition, meaning a condition that was treated or showed symptoms in the 60 days before your coverage start date. We waive this exclusion if you buy this policy within 14 days of your initial trip deposit, insure the full trip cost and are medically able to travel when you buy it.

### 5.2 Other exclusions

We do not cover loss caused by intentional self-injury, use of alcohol or drugs not prescribed by a doctor, participation in professional sports, mountaineering above 4,500 metres, skydiving, travel against a government "do not travel" advisory issued before you bought the policy, or a pandemic that was publicly known before you bought the policy.

## Section 6 — Claims and Conditions

### 6.1 Submitting a claim

You must notify us within 20 days of a loss, or as soon as reasonably possible, and send receipts, proof of trip cost and supporting documents within 90 days.

### 6.2 Free look period

You may cancel within 14 days of buying this policy for a full refund, provided you have not departed and no claim has been made. After that, the premium is not refundable.
