# DriveWell Personal Auto Policy

## Part A — Definitions

### A.1 "Covered auto"

"Covered auto" means any vehicle shown on the declarations page, a private passenger auto or pickup you acquire during the policy period if you ask us to insure it within 30 days, and a temporary substitute auto used while a covered auto is out of service because of breakdown, repair or loss.

### A.2 "Family member"

"Family member" means a person related to you by blood, marriage, domestic partnership or adoption who is a resident of your household, including a ward or foster child.

### A.3 "Rideshare activity"

"Rideshare activity" has three periods. Period 1 begins when a driver logs on to a transportation network company's app, ready to accept ride requests, and ends when a request is accepted. Period 2 runs from accepting a request until the passenger is picked up. Period 3 runs while the passenger is in the vehicle.

## Part B — Liability Coverage

### B.1 Insuring agreement

We pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which an insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident, and we defend the insured at our expense. Our duty to defend ends when the limit of liability has been exhausted.

### B.2 Limits

The limits shown on the declarations page apply per person and per accident for bodily injury and per accident for property damage. The minimum limits we write are $50,000 per person, $100,000 per accident and $50,000 property damage.

### B.3 Rideshare coverage

During Period 1 of rideshare activity, liability coverage applies up to $50,000 per person, $100,000 per accident and $25,000 property damage, as excess over any insurance provided by the transportation network company. We do not provide liability coverage during Periods 2 and 3.

### B.4 Exclusions

We do not provide liability coverage for any insured while using a vehicle to carry goods for a fee, except food delivery in a private passenger auto, or as any other public or livery conveyance.

## Part C — Medical Payments

### C.1 Insuring agreement

We pay reasonable medical and funeral expenses incurred within three years of an accident for bodily injury to an occupant of a covered auto, up to $5,000 per person unless a different limit is shown.

## Part D — Uninsured Motorist Coverage

### D.1 Insuring agreement

We pay compensatory damages an insured is legally entitled to recover from the owner or driver of an uninsured, underinsured or hit-and-run motor vehicle because of bodily injury, up to the uninsured motorist limit shown on the declarations page. A hit-and-run accident must be reported to the police within 24 hours.

## Part E — Physical Damage Coverage

### E.1 Collision

We pay for direct and accidental loss to a covered auto caused by collision with another object or by upset, less the collision deductible. Unless a different amount is shown, the collision deductible is $500.

### E.2 Comprehensive

We pay for direct and accidental loss to a covered auto not caused by collision, including fire, theft, vandalism, hail, flood, falling objects and contact with an animal, less the comprehensive deductible. Unless a different amount is shown, the comprehensive deductible is $250.

### E.3 Glass

Glass breakage is covered under comprehensive. No deductible applies when damaged glass is repaired instead of replaced. The comprehensive deductible applies to glass replacement.

### E.4 Non-owned and rental autos

Physical damage coverage on this policy extends to a private passenger auto you rent for personal use in the United States or Canada for no more than 45 consecutive days. Coverage is the same as the broadest coverage on any covered auto. We also pay loss-of-use charges from the rental company up to $30 per day, with a maximum of $900.

### E.5 Custom equipment

We cover custom equipment permanently installed in a covered auto, such as aftermarket wheels or sound systems, up to $1,500.

### E.6 Exclusions

We do not cover damage due to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical breakdown, tire damage unless caused by a covered loss, or loss to a covered auto during Periods 2 and 3 of rideshare activity.

## Part F — Duties and Conditions

### F.1 Duties after an accident or loss

You must tell us promptly how, when and where the accident or loss happened, and in any case no later than 30 days after it, and give us the names and addresses of any injured persons and witnesses. You must notify the police within 24 hours if a covered auto is stolen.

### F.2 Total loss

If the cost to repair a covered auto exceeds 75% of its actual cash value, we treat it as a total loss and pay actual cash value less the deductible. If you owe more on a loan or lease than we pay, endorsement HM-PA-END-06 (Loan/Lease Gap) covers the difference only if it is attached.

### F.3 Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by notifying us. We may cancel for non-payment of premium with at least 10 days' notice. After the policy has been in effect for 60 days, we may cancel only for non-payment, fraud, or suspension of the driver's licence of a driver who lives with you. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
