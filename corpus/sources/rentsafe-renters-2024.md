# RentSafe Renters Policy

## Section 1 — Agreement and Definitions

### 1.1 Agreement

In return for the premium you pay, Harborline Mutual Insurance Company ("we", "us", "our") agrees to provide the insurance described in this policy to a tenant of a rented residence. This policy, the declarations page and any endorsements form the entire contract between you and us.

### 1.2 "Insured"

"Insured" means you, the named insured shown on the declarations page, and residents of your household who are your relatives or are under the age of 21 and in your care. A roommate is not an insured unless named on the declarations page.

### 1.3 "Residence premises"

"Residence premises" means the apartment, unit or house you rent at the address shown on the declarations page, where you reside.

## Section 2 — Property Coverages

### 2.1 Coverage C — Personal Property

We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. Property kept in a storage unit is covered up to 10% of the Coverage C limit.

### 2.2 Special limits of liability

The following limits apply to each loss for property covered under Coverage C:

- $200 for money, bank notes and coins.
- $1,500 for theft of jewelry, watches and furs.
- $2,500 for theft of firearms.
- $1,500 for bicycles, including electric bicycles.
- $2,500 for electronic devices used primarily for business.

### 2.3 Improvements and betterments

We cover improvements, alterations and additions you made at your own expense to the rented unit, such as fixtures or built-in shelving, up to 10% of the Coverage C limit.

### 2.4 Coverage D — Loss of Use

If a covered loss makes the residence premises unfit to live in, we cover the necessary increase in living expenses for the shortest time required to repair the damage or for your household to settle elsewhere, but no longer than 12 months from the date of loss.

## Section 3 — Perils Insured Against

### 3.1 Named perils

We insure Coverage C property against direct physical loss caused by: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot or civil commotion; aircraft; vehicles; smoke; vandalism; theft; falling objects; weight of ice, snow or sleet; and sudden and accidental discharge of water or steam from a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system or a household appliance.

### 3.2 Theft from a vehicle

Theft of property from an unattended vehicle is covered only if there are visible signs of forced entry. The most we pay for theft from a vehicle is $1,000 per occurrence.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Flood and earth movement

We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, earthquake, landslide or any other earth movement.

### 4.2 Building damage

We do not cover damage to the building, walls, floors, appliances supplied by the landlord or other property owned by the landlord. The landlord's own insurance covers the building.

### 4.3 Pests and gradual damage

We do not cover loss caused by insects, rodents, vermin, mold, or continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of 14 days or more.

## Section 5 — Liability Coverages

### 5.1 Coverage E — Personal Liability

If a claim is made or a suit is brought against an insured for bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence, we pay up to the limit shown on the declarations page and provide a defense at our expense. Unless a different amount is shown, the limit is $100,000 per occurrence.

### 5.2 Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

We pay necessary medical expenses incurred within three years of an accident causing bodily injury to a guest at the residence premises. The limit is $1,000 per person. This coverage does not apply to you or regular residents of your household.

### 5.3 Damage to the landlord's property

We cover property damage caused by an insured to the rented unit, such as accidental fire or water overflow from a bathtub, up to $10,000 per occurrence. Damage caused intentionally or by neglect is not covered.

### 5.4 Animal liability

Personal liability for bodily injury caused by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured is limited to $25,000 per occurrence.

## Section 6 — Deductibles and Conditions

### 6.1 Deductible

Each loss under Section 2 is subject to the deductible shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the deductible is $500. No deductible applies to Section 5.

### 6.2 Your duties after loss

You must give us notice no later than 60 days after the date of loss, notify the police in case of theft and provide an inventory of damaged property with receipts or photos where available.

### 6.3 Loss settlement

Personal property is settled at replacement cost, up to the Coverage C limit, provided you replace the property within 180 days of the loss. Until the property is replaced, we pay actual cash value.

### 6.4 Moving to a new residence

If you move within the policy period, Coverage C applies at both locations for 30 days from the date you begin to move. You must tell us your new address within those 30 days.

### 6.5 Cancellation

You may cancel this policy at any time by notifying us in writing or by phone. We may cancel for non-payment of premium by giving you at least 10 days' notice. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
