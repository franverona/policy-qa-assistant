# Roadside Assistance Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Policies this endorsement attaches to

This endorsement can be attached to DriveWell personal auto policies (HM-PA-2023 and HM-PA-2024) and Roadster motorcycle policies (HM-MC-2024).

## Endorsement Terms

### 2 Covered services

When a covered vehicle breaks down, we pay for the following services arranged through the Harborline roadside line:

- Towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, or up to 15 miles to a facility you choose.
- Jump start, flat tyre change using your spare, and lockout service.
- Delivery of fuel, oil or water. The cost of the fuel itself is not covered.
- Winching when the vehicle is stuck within 100 feet of a public road.

### 3 Limits

Lockout service is covered up to $100 per call, including a locksmith if needed. Each covered vehicle may use up to 4 service calls per policy term.

### 4 Services you arrange yourself

If you cannot reach the Harborline roadside line, we reimburse reasonable charges for covered services up to $150 per call. You must send the receipt within 60 days of the service.

### 5 Not covered

We do not pay for repairs, parts, storage, or towing from a repair shop, or for services in off-road locations, during races or for vehicles stuck in snow on an unploughed road.

### 6 Effect on your premium

Roadside assistance calls are not treated as claims and do not affect your premium or claims history.
