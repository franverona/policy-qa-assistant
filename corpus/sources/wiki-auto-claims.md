# Auto Claims Handling Guide

## Glass claims

Windscreen and window claims go to our glass partner, ClearView Glass Network, which can book a mobile repair within 48 hours. On HM-PA-2024 policies, glass repair has no deductible; replacement uses the comprehensive deductible. On HM-PA-2023 policies, the comprehensive deductible applies to both. Tier 1 agents can book glass claims directly without an adjuster.

## Towing after an accident

If the vehicle is not drivable after an accident, tow it to one of our preferred repair shops when possible. Preferred shops give a lifetime guarantee on their repairs. If the customer has roadside endorsement HM-PA-END-05, the first tow does not count as one of their 4 service calls when it follows a collision claim.

## Choosing a repair shop

Customers may choose any licensed repair shop. We do not require them to use a preferred shop, and agents must never say that coverage depends on it.

## Rental cars

A rental car is paid only if the customer has rental reimbursement endorsement HM-PA-END-03, or if the other driver's insurer has accepted liability. Check the endorsement before promising a rental.

## Total loss

The adjuster decides whether a vehicle is a total loss. The threshold is 75% of actual cash value on HM-PA-2024 and 80% on HM-PA-2023. When a vehicle is a total loss, the customer must send the title, all keys and a lien release if there is a loan. Payment is made within 10 business days of receiving the documents.

## Hit-and-run and uninsured drivers

Hit-and-run accidents must be reported to the police within 24 hours for uninsured motorist coverage to apply. Ask for the police report number at FNOL. If the customer has not reported it, tell them to do so immediately.

## Accidents abroad

DriveWell covers accidents in the United States and Canada only. If the customer drove into Mexico, coverage does not apply and they need Mexican auto insurance bought locally.
