# Home-Based Business Endorsement

## Applicability

### 1 Eligible businesses

This endorsement can be attached to HomeShield (HM-HO-2024) and CondoGuard (HM-CO-2024) policies for a business that you own and run from the residence premises and that meets all of these conditions:

- It has no more than 3 employees, not counting you and family members.
- Its annual gross revenue is no more than $250,000.
- Customer visits to the residence premises average no more than 10 per week.

### 2 Ineligible businesses

This endorsement is not available for daycare, firearms sales, manufacturing, food preparation for sale, short-term rentals, or any professional services such as legal, medical, accounting or engineering advice.

## Endorsement Terms

### 3 Business property

We cover business property, including equipment, inventory and records, up to $10,000 while on the residence premises and up to $2,500 while away from the residence premises. These limits replace the $5,000 special limit for business electronics in the policy.

### 4 Loss of business income

If a covered loss to the residence premises stops your business, we pay the business income you lose and the extra expenses you incur, for no longer than 12 months, up to $25,000.

### 5 Business liability

We pay damages for bodily injury, property damage and product liability arising out of the business, up to $300,000 per occurrence and $600,000 in total per policy year.

### 6 Not covered

This endorsement does not cover professional errors and omissions, data breaches, injury to your employees (which requires workers' compensation insurance) or business vehicles.
