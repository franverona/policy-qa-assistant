# CondoGuard Unit Owners Policy

## Section 1 — Agreement and Definitions

### 1.1 Agreement

In return for the premium you pay, Harborline Mutual Insurance Company ("we", "us", "our") agrees to provide the insurance described in this policy to the owner of a residential condominium unit. This policy, the declarations page and any endorsements form the entire contract between you and us.

### 1.2 "Association"

"Association" means the condominium association, cooperative board or homeowners association that owns or manages the common elements of the building in which your unit is located.

### 1.3 "Master policy"

"Master policy" means the insurance bought by the association that covers the building and common elements.

## Section 2 — Property Coverages

### 2.1 Coverage A — Unit

We cover the parts of your unit that the association bylaws make your responsibility to insure, including interior walls, floor coverings, cabinets, fixtures and improvements you made. Unless the declarations page shows a different amount, the Coverage A limit is $5,000.

### 2.2 Coverage C — Personal Property

We cover personal property owned or used by you while it is anywhere in the world. The special limits of liability for money, jewelry, firearms and business electronics are the same as in HomeShield form HM-HO-2024, clause 2.4.

### 2.3 Coverage D — Loss of Use

If a covered loss makes your unit unfit to live in, we cover additional living expenses for no longer than 12 months from the date of loss. This includes loss of use caused by damage to a neighbouring unit or the common elements that prevents access to your unit.

### 2.4 Loss assessment

We pay your share of an assessment charged by the association during the policy period because of direct loss to property owned collectively by all unit owners, caused by a peril insured against. The most we pay is $1,000 per assessment unless a higher limit, up to $50,000, is shown on the declarations page. We do not pay assessments for the association's master policy deductible above $10,000.

## Section 3 — Perils Insured Against

### 3.1 Unit

We insure Coverage A property against direct physical loss unless the loss is excluded in Section 4.

### 3.2 Personal property

We insure Coverage C property against the same named perils listed in HomeShield form HM-HO-2024, clause 3.2.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### 4.1 Flood and earth movement

We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, earthquake or any other earth movement.

### 4.2 Wear, tear and maintenance

We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, deterioration, mold, or continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of 14 days or more.

### 4.3 Property covered by the master policy

We do not cover any part of the building that the master policy covers, except to the extent the master policy does not pay because of its deductible. In that case Coverage A pays up to its limit.

### 4.4 Short-term rentals

We do not cover loss to personal property while your unit is rented to others for fewer than 30 consecutive days.

## Section 5 — Liability Coverages

### 5.1 Coverage E — Personal Liability

We pay damages an insured is legally liable for because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence, up to the limit shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the limit is $300,000 per occurrence.

### 5.2 Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

We pay necessary medical expenses for a guest injured in your unit, up to $2,000 per person.

## Section 6 — Deductibles and Conditions

### 6.1 Deductible

Each loss under Section 2 is subject to the deductible shown on the declarations page. Unless a different amount is shown, the deductible is $1,000. A loss assessment claim is subject to a deductible of $250.

### 6.2 Your duties after loss

You must give us notice no later than 60 days after the date of loss, tell the association about any damage to the unit or common elements, and give us a copy of the association bylaws and master policy declarations if we ask.

### 6.3 Loss settlement

Coverage A losses are settled at replacement cost if repairs are completed within 12 months of the date of loss. Personal property is settled at actual cash value unless endorsement HM-END-02 is attached.

### 6.4 Other insurance

If the master policy also covers a loss, this policy is excess over the master policy.

### 6.5 Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel for non-payment of premium with at least 10 days' notice, or for any other permitted reason with at least 30 days' notice. Any unearned premium is refunded pro rata.
