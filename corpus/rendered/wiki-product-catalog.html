Product Catalog and Policy Form Codes

Purpose

This page lists every personal lines product Harborline Mutual sells, with its policy form code, so agents can find the right wording quickly. The form code is printed under the title of every policy and on the declarations page next to "Policy form". Last reviewed by Product Management in May 2024.

Property products

  • HomeShield Homeowners: HM-HO-2024 (current edition) and HM-HO-2022 (previous edition, still in force on some policies).
  • RentSafe Renters: HM-RN-2024.
  • CondoGuard Unit Owners: HM-CO-2024.
  • Landlord Dwelling: HM-DP-2024, for one- to four-family homes rented on leases of 30 days or more.

Vehicle products

  • DriveWell Personal Auto: HM-PA-2024 (current edition) and HM-PA-2023 (previous edition).
  • Roadster Motorcycle: HM-MC-2024.
  • Seaworthy Watercraft: HM-WC-2024, for boats up to 32 feet.

Other products

  • Harbor Personal Umbrella: HM-UM-2024, limits from $1,000,000 to $5,000,000.
  • PawGuard Pet Health: HM-PET-2024, dogs and cats only.
  • Voyager Travel Protection: HM-TR-2024, single trips of up to 90 days.

Endorsements

  • HM-END-02 Personal Property Replacement Cost.
  • HM-END-04 Water Backup and Sump Discharge.
  • HM-END-07 Home-Based Business.
  • HM-END-09 Scheduled Personal Property.
  • HM-PA-END-03 Auto Rental Reimbursement.
  • HM-PA-END-05 Roadside Assistance.
  • HM-PA-END-06 Loan/Lease Gap.

Products we do not sell

Harborline Mutual does not sell flood insurance, earthquake insurance, life insurance, health insurance, commercial auto or business owners policies. If a customer asks for one of these, tell them we do not offer it and suggest they speak to an independent agent. Do not recommend a specific competitor.
