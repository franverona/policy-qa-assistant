Adding and Removing Endorsements

Who can make changes

Tier 1 agents can add or remove HM-END-02, HM-PA-END-03 and HM-PA-END-05 after verifying the caller. Adding HM-END-04, HM-END-07, HM-END-09 or HM-PA-END-06 needs underwriting review, so Tier 1 agents take the request and send it to the underwriting queue (UW queue). Underwriting replies within 2 business days.

Effective dates

Endorsements start at 12:01 a.m. on the day after the change is made, never on the same day. Customers cannot backdate an endorsement to cover a loss that has already happened. Water backup HM-END-04 also has a 10-day waiting period when it is added mid-term.

Water backup eligibility

HM-END-04 is not available for homes with a sewer backup claim in the past 5 years, or for addresses in a declared CAT event area while the binding moratorium is active.

Scheduling valuables

To schedule an item under HM-END-09, the customer must email a recent appraisal or receipt for items over $5,000. Items up to $5,000 can be scheduled from a description and photo. Explain that scheduled items have no deductible and cover accidental loss, unlike the standard theft limits.

Home business eligibility questions

Before sending HM-END-07 to underwriting, ask how many employees the business has, its annual revenue, what it does and how many customers visit each week. If the business provides professional advice, tell the customer HM-END-07 is not available and they need a separate business policy, which Harborline does not sell.

Removing endorsements

When an endorsement is removed mid-term, the premium for it is refunded pro rata. Removing HM-PA-END-06 requires proof that the loan or lease has been paid off or the vehicle sold.
